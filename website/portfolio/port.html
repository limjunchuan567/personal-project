These experiences have shown me how vital teamwork and clear communication are in professional environments where collaboration among professionals is essential for providing the best results.

Disability Awareness and Experiential Carnival

Lead Organizer & Experiential Activity Designer

As an organiser for the national-level Disability Awareness and Experiential Carnival at Boon Lay Community Club, I demonstrated innovation, adaptability, and resilience by designing interactive, hands-on activities that moved beyond passive awareness to foster genuine empathy, understanding, and inclusivity. My team and I developed experiential simulations—such as navigating spaces blindfolded, communicating without speaking, and playing wheelchair soccer—allowing participants to step into the shoes of disabled individuals and experience firsthand the physical and emotional barriers they encounter daily.

To ensure excellence, I meticulously oversaw logistics, manpower, and resources, maintaining integrity through open communication with external partners and effective collaboration with the Boon Lay Community Club to navigate planning challenges. This project successfully sparked action, inspiring participants to promote accessibility and create inclusive spaces where everyone feels valued.

Reading Wonders Literacy Programme

Primary 1 Student Reading Mentorship

Driven by a commitment to equal opportunity, I led a literacy programme for Primary 1 students at West Grove Primary School who were struggling with reading. I realised that education is the ultimate door-opener, so I used a flexible teaching style—using pictures and storytelling to match each child’s interests.

This experience taught me the true meaning of kindness and patience; I learned that celebrating small wins, like a student decoding their first sentence, is the key to building confidence. Beyond just teaching skills, I saw how education gives children the agency to succeed. This work strengthened my adaptability and my desire to use compassionate leadership to ensure every individual has the support they need to reach their full potential.

Project Reminiscence (Elderly Engagement)

National Orchid Garden Outings with SAGE Counselling Centre

Through my involvement in Project Reminiscence, I spearheaded outings to the National Orchid Garden for elderly individuals, effectively merging my passion for community service with a dedicated focus on promoting physical, mental, and social well-being. By coordinating closely with volunteers from the SAGE Counselling Centre, I honed critical leadership and communication skills while ensuring every participant felt comfortable and deeply engaged.

This hands-on experience provided a profound understanding of holistic care, as I assisted those with mobility challenges by navigating wheelchairs across slopes and offering constant reassurance. Witnessing how meaningful social interaction and inclusivity directly uplift mental and emotional health has reinforced my empathy and commitment to supporting the well-being of others.

School Activities & CCA Highlights

BPGHS Chinese Orchestra // EXCO & Performance Journey

As a dedicated member of the BPGHS Chinese Orchestra, I consistently demonstrated my passion for Chinese orchestral music through active participation and commitment. Despite initial setbacks, including the challenge of learning a new instrument and mastering musical scores from scratch, I did not make the cut after my first audition for the Singapore Youth Festival (SYF) Arts Presentation 2021 team. Rather than allowing this to discourage me, I treated it as an opportunity for growth, dedicating myself to disciplined practice and proactively seeking guidance from seniors to refine my technique, strengthen my instrument mastery, and improve my ability to synchronise with the ensemble. Through perseverance and determination, I successfully re-auditioned and became an integral member of the team.

Beyond my personal progress, I also contributed meaningfully as an EXCO member, where I played a key role in fostering teamwork and encouraging my peers to work towards shared goals. I actively participated in numerous school performances, including the annual Chinese New Year and Mid-Autumn Festival celebrations, Open House showcases, and the CCA Roadshow, further demonstrating my commitment to the orchestra. Through this experience, I not only deepened my appreciation for music but also strengthened my resilience, adaptability, and ability to collaborate effectively under pressure, culminating in achieving a Certificate of Distinction at the SYF Arts Presentation.

My Philosophy & Vision

Driven by a fascination with complex problem-solving, I have focused my academic journey on mastering the languages of the future, including Python and C++. However, I believe technology is most powerful when it serves a human purpose. My study and practice has equipped me with the analytical tools to build efficient systems, but my work beyond the screen defines my mission.

Organising the Disability Awareness and Experiential Carnival taught me that true leadership lies in creating inclusive spaces through logistics and empathy. Whether tutoring children in literacy or accompanying the elderly at the National Orchid Garden, I strive to bridge gaps in accessibility. I aim to leverage my technical expertise and discipline to develop innovative solutions for social good—ensuring that progress is accessible to everyone.
