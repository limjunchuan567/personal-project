Experiences

During my internship at BorgWarner, I developed crucial 21st-century competencies like collaboration and communication skills. Working with a cross-functional team, I learned about the importance of fostering open communication and active listening among team members. The collaborative approach allowed us to find effective solutions and improve our processes. I helped address process inefficiencies by fostering open communication and encouraging active listening among team members.

I learned that success depends not just on technical expertise, but also on the ability to work well with others and build trust. I took part in BorgWarner’s engineering internship where I learned about the engineering processes, worked with mentors, and improved my problem-solving skills. These experiences have shown me how vital teamwork and clear communication are in professional environments where collaboration among professionals is essential for providing the best results.

As an organiser for the national-level Disability Awareness and Experiential Carnival at Boon Lay Community Club, I demonstrated innovation, adaptability, and resilience by designing interactive, hands-on activities that moved beyond passive awareness to foster genuine empathy, understanding, and inclusivity. My team and I developed experiential simulations—such as navigating spaces blindfolded, communicating without speaking, and playing wheelchair soccer—allowing participants to step into the shoes of disabled individuals and experience firsthand the physical and emotional barriers they encounter daily. To ensure excellence, I meticulously oversaw logistics, manpower, and resources, maintaining integrity through open communication with external partners and effective collaboration with the Boon Lay Community Club to navigate planning challenges. This project successfully sparked action, inspiring participants to promote accessibility and create inclusive spaces where everyone feels valued. Ultimately, balancing these responsibilities with other commitments sharpened my time management, solidifying my dedication to equitable treatment and the creation of fair, supportive communities.

Driven by a commitment to equal opportunity, I led a literacy programme for Primary 1 students at West Grove Primary School who were struggling with reading. I realised that education is the ultimate door-opener, so I used a flexible teaching style—using pictures and storytelling to match each child’s interests. This experience taught me the true meaning of kindness and patience; I learned that celebrating small wins, like a student decoding their first sentence, is the key to building confidence. Beyond just teaching skills, I saw how education gives children the agency to succeed. This work strengthened my adaptability and my desire to use compassionate leadership to ensure every individual has the support they need to reach their full potential.

Through my involvement in Project Reminiscence, I spearheaded outings to the National Orchid Garden for elderly individuals, effectively merging my passion for community service with a dedicated focus on promoting physical, mental, and social well-being. By coordinating closely with volunteers from the SAGE Counselling Centre, I honed critical leadership and communication skills while ensuring every participant felt comfortable and deeply engaged.

This hands-on experience provided a profound understanding of holistic care, as I assisted those with mobility challenges by navigating wheelchairs across slopes and offering constant reassurance. Witnessing how meaningful social interaction and inclusivity directly uplift mental and emotional health has reinforced my empathy and commitment to supporting the well-being of others. These rewarding interactions—centred on listening to their stories, sharing conversations, and fostering connections with nature—upheld a culture of inclusivity and strengthened my resolve to pursue a career where I can continue to improve lives through compassionate, comprehensive service.

CCA

As a dedicated member of the BPGHS Chinese Orchestra, I consistently demonstrated my passion for Chinese orchestral music through active participation and commitment. Despite initial setbacks, including the challenge of learning a new instrument and mastering musical scores from scratch, I did not make the cut after my first audition for the Singapore Youth Festival (SYF) Arts Presentation 2021 team. Rather than allowing this to discourage me, I treated it as an opportunity for growth, dedicating myself to disciplined practice and proactively seeking guidance from seniors to refine my technique, strengthen my instrument mastery, and improve my ability to synchronise with the ensemble. Through perseverance and determination, I successfully re-auditioned and became an integral member of the team.

Beyond my personal progress, I also contributed meaningfully as an EXCO member, where I played a key role in fostering teamwork and encouraging my peers to work towards shared goals. I actively participated in numerous school performances, including the annual Chinese New Year and Mid-Autumn Festival celebrations, Open House showcases, and the CCA Roadshow, further demonstrating my commitment to the orchestra. This journey of resilience and leadership, together with the collective efforts of the team, culminated in us achieving a Certificate of Distinction at the SYF Arts Presentation. Through this experience, I not only deepened my appreciation for music but also strengthened my resilience, adaptability, and ability to collaborate effectively under pressure.

As a member of the school media team, I wrote articles about school events such as Teachers’ Day, RVmun, and YLEAD. I conducted interviews with many people and participated in these events to gain personal insights. I also collaborated with various CCAs, such as Venture Scouts and HACAs, to publish their articles, forming new connections and improving my writing skills in the process. I learned new writing techniques to make my articles more interesting and developed my communication skills to ensure interviewees felt comfortable during interviews. Through this experience, I learned 21st-century competencies such as self-awareness, self-management, responsible decision-making, and social awareness. Interviewing involved empathetic listening and understanding others’ perspectives and experiences. I learned when to be quiet and when to ask questions, developing empathy, cultural sensitivity, and a broader worldview that is essential for building strong relationships, resolving conflicts, and collaborating effectively with diverse groups. Crafting insightful questions and analysing responses enhanced my critical thinking skills, enabling me to evaluate information, identify patterns, and draw conclusions more effectively. Through my CCA involvement, I overcame my lack of confidence and communication challenges, becoming a more sociable and friendlier person.
